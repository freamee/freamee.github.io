Multiplayer Winery

A shared production experience where players purchase a winery, connect machinery, process ingredients and manage the business together.

Features​

Environment

The winery world

Winery ownership

Players can purchase their own winery and invite others to help operate the business.

Custom production equipment

Purpose-built assets include pumps, containers, a bottling machine and other equipment used throughout production.

Instanced winery interior

Each winery provides a dedicated interior where the complete production process can be managed.

Immersive sound design

Custom sounds add clear feedback to machinery, containers and other interactive equipment.

Player experience

Production workflow

Connected machinery

Connect equipment such as pumps and barrels to build a practical, hands-on production workflow.

Fermentation and processing

Move products through a complete production pipeline from preparation to the finished result.

Condition and quality

Production progress, equipment condition and product quality all influence the final outcome.

Configuration

Management and configuration

Winery laptop

Manage workers, purchase tools and oversee the day-to-day operation from one interface.

Player permissions

Choose who can access the property, manage locks and perform specific winery tasks.

Synchronized multiplayer

Production progress, machinery and shared activity remain synchronized for everyone inside the winery.

Optimized performance

Efficient point-based handling keeps the resource lightweight, even on larger servers.

Flexible configuration

Adjust production values, economy settings and other gameplay behavior to match your server.

Localization

Translate the resource through straightforward JSON locale files.

Immersive Greenhouse

A fully multiplayer greenhouse experience where players own and upgrade their properties, cultivate crops, manage workers and storage, and build a thriving farming business together.

Features​

Property and infrastructure

Build and expand the greenhouse

Buy and own your greenhouse

Purchase a greenhouse through a fully custom shared lobby, then manage ownership, rename the property and control its lock state.

Custom interiors and props

Explore custom greenhouse interiors filled with purpose-built farming equipment, tools, storage crates and interactive objects.

Greenhouse expansion

Upgrade through Small, Medium, Large and Massive sizes, supporting up to 160 planting spots across eight sections.

Section upgrades

Improve individual sections with tarps, UV lighting, ventilation and water systems. Upgrades consume materials and visibly transform the interior.

Irrigation system

Control irrigation through an interactive switch, with synchronized sprinkler effects in upgraded sections.

Storage system

Manage supplies, seeds, harvested goods and upgrade materials through shared storage with configurable capacities.

Management computer

Manage the greenhouse, workers, upgrades, storage, purchases, reputation and statistics from an interactive in-world computer.

Farming and progression

Cultivation and progression

Interactive farming system

Prepare soil, dig, plant seeds, water crops and harvest with dedicated tools, animations and sound effects.

Seven crop types

Grow tomatoes, blueberries, strawberries, watermelons, peppers, coca and cannabis, each with its own configured properties.

Dynamic crop growth

Watch crops develop through visual growth stages with changing models, produce visibility, health and water requirements.

Seed purchasing

Purchase different seed types directly through the greenhouse management interface.

Reputation system

Build reputation with ten NPC contacts, each offering unique requests, preferred products and material rewards.

Delivery missions

Accept requests, transport goods with a delivery vehicle and return the vehicle to complete the job.

Statistics system

Track planting, harvesting and other farming activities across the greenhouse operation.

Management and integration

Multiplayer administration

Fully multiplayer

Multiple players can work inside the same greenhouse with synchronized crops, interactions and property states.

Workers and permissions

Add workers, assign roles and configure individual permissions with the Owner, Co-Owner, Manager, Supervisor, Senior Worker and Worker roles.

Optional proficiency integration

Supports the separately sold proficiency resource with five farming skills, XP progression, levels and milestones.

Server-client synchronization

Server-managed greenhouse data keeps interactions and multiplayer states synchronized for every player.

Extensive configuration

Balance properties, seeds, storage, missions and other gameplay systems through the config file.

Localization

English and Hungarian locale files are included.

Additional features

Includes many additional details, interactions and smaller features beyond those listed above.
